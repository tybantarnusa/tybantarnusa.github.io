104th Term — Episode 11

Hasunosora 104th Term (Main Story) — Episode 11 story summary & archive

▶ Buka web interaktif Link! Like!

A flashback shows Ceras scouting ~300 schools to save Mizukawa from closure and recruiting inexperienced Izumi through sheer passion, pledging herself to the cause. In the present, as Hasunosora preps the playoff, Ceras and Izumi deliver devastating news: Mizukawa's closure is finalized and club activities are suspended, forcing their withdrawal from Love Live!. The Hasunosora members mourn their rivals' loss even amid their own momentum, setting up Ceras and Izumi's search for a new home.
