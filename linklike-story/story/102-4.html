102nd Term — Episode 4

Hasunosora 102nd Term (Voiced Novel (Side Story)) — Episode 4 story summary & archive

▶ Buka web interaktif Link! Like!

On the day of the April debut, Sachi surprises the club with replica costumes of the legendary Dream Believers who once won Love Live!, moving Kozue to tears while Megumi stays cynical before admitting the quality. Tsuzuri stuns everyone with her natural stage presence at the Music Hall, where DOLLCHESTRA performs 'Sparkly Spot' and Megumi watches Sachi and Tsuzuri grow into a real unit. As Kozue's solo 'Reflection in the Mirror' approaches, Megumi feels creeping anxiety, but when Kozue freezes on stage, Megumi's support and the trio's trust pull her through, proving to Megumi that school idols can truly be the 'light' Kozue always believed in.
