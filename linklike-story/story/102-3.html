102nd Term — Episode 3

Hasunosora 102nd Term (Voiced Novel (Side Story)) — Episode 3 story summary & archive

▶ Buka web interaktif Link! Like!

Kozue reflects on how she dreamed of Love Live! since first grade and fought her prestigious musical family to attend Hasunosora, even as she grows frustrated watching Megumi's casual popularity at school. During practice Sachi trains all three units at once, revealing she is the chairwoman's granddaughter and a prodigy in her own right, but the tension peaks when Megumi suddenly stops coming. Kozue discovers Megumi watched her old TV show and realized the audience has moved on and forgotten her; devastated by the fleeting nature of fame, Megumi decides to quit the club and chase her old career, calling school idols a waste of time. Until Kozue confronts her in the dorm, arguing that their bond matters more than any industry comeback.
