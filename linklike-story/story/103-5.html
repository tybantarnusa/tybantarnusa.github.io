103rd Term — Episode 5

Hasunosora 103rd Term (Main Story) — Episode 5 story summary & archive

Tsuzuri gives Kaho and Kozue acorns as thanks for helping her realize she'd neglected Sayaka, then during DOLLCHESTRA practice she critiques Sayaka's lack of 'presence,' pushing Sayaka to open up about her struggles. Sayaka reveals her sister's career-ending injury and her own fear of inadequacy, so Tsuzuri drags her to a Kanazawa market to work at a seafood bowl shop run by acquaintance Reika. Sayaka initially fails to greet customers with warmth, but Reika explains Tsuzuri's role is simply being a 'billboard' whose presence makes people happy, triggering Sayaka's realization that she must reciprocate the community's support. She delivers a high-energy greeting, impresses Kaho (who streams it), and learns the senior-junior market tradition Tsuzuri insisted she experience, finally accepting her own identity over imitating her sister and resolving to decide her future.
