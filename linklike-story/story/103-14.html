103rd Term — Episode 14

Hasunosora 103rd Term (Main Story) — Episode 14 story summary & archive

Training for the Hokuriku Regionals, Kaho suggests performing as one six-member group to avoid units competing, but Kozue firmly refuses, valuing each unit's unique identity and her bond with Kaho in Cerise Bouquet. Then Sachi delivers grave news: a 'restricted' faction proposes an internet ban that would kill the club's School Idol Connect streaming. With Sachi's council term ending in December, Kaho proposes a gamble, perform the qualifier via remote stream and win using the very tech the school wants to ban, giving the permissive faculty leverage. The crisis deepens when a data cap and 'outing ban' block off-campus practice, forcing the club to fight for its existence.
