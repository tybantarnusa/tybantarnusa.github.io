Rurino formally joins and quickly becomes the club's energetic spark, but she grows confused that Megumi, who first inspired her, has gone distant and stopped visiting. Kozue and Tsuzuri finally reveal the truth: at last year's Rindou Festival, Megumi fell and was injured; though her body healed, the trauma left a psychological block where her legs freeze on stage, so she quit performing and turned to video production. Determined to help, Rurino leads Kaho and Sayaka to Megumi's dorm, but Megumi greets them warmly yet deflects, claiming exhaustion from editing and gently turning them away, leaving Rurino hurt and more resolved to reach her.
