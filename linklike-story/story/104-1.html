104th Term — Episode 1

Hasunosora 104th Term (Main Story) — Episode 1 story summary & archive

The new school year opens with Kaho oversleeping and rushing in, delighted to find Sayaka and Rurino in her same class as second-years; self-conscious she hasn't 'grown up,' she adopts a stiff formal speech to look like a mature senior, amusing her friends. As upperclassmen they discuss recruiting freshmen and their units' differing philosophies, Sayaka noting DOLLCHESTRA won't chase recruits, but a quiet first-year, Ginko Momose, is already lingering near the club room absorbing its 'tradition,' and when Kaho clumsily serves tea and Ginko's notebook spills to reveal dozens of original costume designs, Kaho impulsively invites her to join Cerise Bouquet, setting the recruitment season in motion.
