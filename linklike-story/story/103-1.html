103rd Term — Episode 1

Hasunosora 103rd Term (Main Story) — Episode 1 story summary & archive

Kaho Hinoshita, raised sickly and overprotected, arrives at Hasunosora bursting with dreams of freedom but finds the secluded campus and cold bus-mate Sayaka crushing her spirit. Rescued from a school otter by Kozue, she is dragged to watch the School Idol Club perform with Tsuzuri, an experience that awakens Sayaka's desire to join. Kozue recruits Kaho as logistics manager for the upcoming Welcome Live. As the transfer deadline looms, Kozue shares how her own musical family opposed her dream and urges Kaho to find her own reason to bloom. Kaho tears up her transfer application, officially joins the club, and performs at the Welcome Live alongside Kozue, finally claiming her place at Hasunosora.
