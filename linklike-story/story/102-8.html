102nd Term — Episode 8

Hasunosora 102nd Term (Voiced Novel (Side Story)) — Episode 8 story summary & archive

▶ Buka web interaktif Link! Like!

The day after their successful Nadeshiko Festival performance, the trio celebrates with senior Sachi in the clubroom over their favorite drinks, their bond now effortless while Kozue still flusters using first names. Sachi expresses pride in the concert and a quiet wish to someday perform alongside them as a full four-member group, and reveals the student body has already nicknamed them the 'Hasu no Daisankaku' (Great Triangle): three first-magnitude stars (Kozue the musical elite, Tsuzuri the dance prodigy, Megumi the former star) whose combined light defines the 102nd term.
