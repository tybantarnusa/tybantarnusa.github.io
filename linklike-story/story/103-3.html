103rd Term — Episode 3

Hasunosora 103rd Term (Main Story) — Episode 3 story summary & archive

Kozue sets an ambitious goal, first place at an upcoming city-wide school idol event, and puts Kaho through a grueling training regimen based on her own middle-school routine, confiscating Kaho's manga as motivation. Pushed too hard, Kaho secretly practices at night, strains her leg, and lies about it at the live; when she falls backstage, Kozue is injured shielding her, and Megumi Fujishima appears to encourage Kaho. Ashamed, Kaho confesses her dishonesty, and after Sayaka reframes her 'selfishness' as sincere effort, Kaho discovers the legacy 'School Idol Notebooks,' reads Kozue's private entries revealing how happy mentoring makes her, and reconciles with Kozue in the Big Warehouse. The two officially form Cerise Bouquet, debut at their live, win April's Monthly MVP, and Megumi, now on hiatus, streams an intro praising Kaho's 'sun-like' spirit.
